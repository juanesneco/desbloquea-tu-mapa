## The Tutorial Phase (Childhood)

When you start the game Earth, you're dropped straight into the tutorial phase, and the developers call this childhood. You learn the basics: how to interact, survive, and get by. But the thing is, this tutorial barely scratches the surface of what you'll need to know to play Earth really well and level up your character.

In most games, when you choose a character, you get to break down all their strengths and weaknesses so you can plan accordingly. But in Earth, that is not how it works. The deep traits, the strengths, and the flaws that your character has are kept hidden from you on purpose. You don't get any guide telling you what you're naturally good at or where you might struggle. The developers designed it this way: you have to dig, you have to work, you've got to explore and understand who your character really is.

Most players who play Earth spend their entire lives without ever fully knowing their character's true potential. They get stuck playing Earth on the surface level, and they miss out on the depth that could change the way they experience the entire game.

## Glitches: Background Programs and Entropy

Here's one of the biggest things that nobody tells you when you start playing Earth: your character is probably running way too many programs at one time. If you imagine having 50 tabs open on your browser all the time... so the game developers turned off the open programs alert by default, so most players don't even realize that it's happening.

These programs keep running in the background. They cause your character to lag, and they reduce our critical abilities. Our focus and our overall performance just goes way down in Earth. These background programs could be anything from unresolved tasks, emotional baggage that your character tries to hold on to, distractions, or habits that just don't level up your character at all.

Then we get into entropy. This is the silent killer. If you don't keep revisiting your systems—how you're earning in-game points and coins, what you're focusing on, and how you're playing—you're going to start trending toward entropy. This is true for all video games. Entropy is the slow drift into chaos and disorder that stops your character from focusing on what really matters.

To beat entropy, you've got to study Earth. You have to study the mechanics of this game, its rules, its hidden systems. The more you understand how the game really works, the better you can control your character's progress. But let's be very real here: most players will not put in the time, which makes leveling up fairly easy compared to most games.

## The Five Key Mechanics

There are five key mechanics that no amount of AI or any kind of automation can really take over, and these are the pillars of real success on Earth. Every character has to master them.

### 1. Connections and Love

Building relationships with other characters is one of the most powerful mechanics in the game, and they're going to really pay off for you in the long run.

### 2. Production

Meaningful work is a core mechanic in the Earth game, and this isn't just about grinding for coins. It's about developing purpose. So what you produce, whether it's physical or intellectual or creative, gives your character a reason to keep playing the game. It's super important.

### 3. Mindset

How your character thinks is everything in this game. Your internal game is just as important as the external. There's no AI that can replace your ability to grow and adapt mentally. So choose good missions and good quests to take your character on.

### 4. Character Health

Monitoring your character's health is super important for taking the game's quests on and challenges. This isn't just about physical health. It's about keeping your character in the best shape mentally and emotionally and physically. They have a lot of crap that you have to monitor, but it's totally worth it. A healthy character performs way better, making it easier to get success and earn coins in the game.

But it does get tricky. The game is filled with instant pleasures that look like you're leveling up but give you a placebo. These are things that give your character a quick boost but there's no long-term benefit. Enjoyment, on the other hand, comes from achieving meaningful goals and staying aligned with your purpose.

### 5. Understanding the Game (Meta Mechanic)

The final goal of the developers is to get you to understand the game: what is the purpose of the game? This is the meta mechanic of the game. Studying the game itself—the more you understand the rules, the mechanics, and how they interact—the better you're going to play this game. You can't outsource this to AI. It's the difference between just surviving in Earth and truly thriving and leveling up your character like crazy in Earth.

## The NPC Trap

When it comes to distraction mode, there's something called the NPC trap. The developers added a trick to convert real players into NPCs, which is non-playable characters. If you're not familiar with NPCs, they're basically the characters in video games that don't have any real control over their actions. They just kind of repeat these routines over and over without any purpose or growth, like a shopkeeper or something like that.

In Earth, this trap is very real, and it's right in front of us. Every time that your character gets sucked into social media, spends hours doom scrolling, politics, and basically you let the character get consumed by whatever media is making them pay attention to, you're walking right into NPC territory.

One of the other ways they do this is by convincing you to keep learning and gathering information by taking game tutorials and never using that information on quests. Lots of game tutorials out there. Most people don't use them. They get addicted to the game tutorials.

The developers don't want every player reaching the higher levels of development, so they flood the entire game with these distractions and they make sure a lot of characters never get past a certain point. It's pretty brilliant. They seduce people with the easy stuff, and before they know it, they've become an NPC just following trends and distractions that are out there.

So all characters are prone to this, so be careful if you decide to try out Earth.

### How to Hack the NPC Trap

Here's the way to hack that: the more you force your character to focus on what really matters—your decade, your year, your monthly goals—the less likely you are to fall into this trap. So keep your eyes on the long term. You level up your character a lot faster, and you get to complete missions and quests instead of wasting your character in low energy loops. They use low energy because it conserves memory processing for the game.

At this point, you start seeing how much of the game is designed to derail you and how many people around you are stuck in the loop. The difference between a real player and an NPC is that the NPC reacts and the player chooses what they want to do. Most of them don't even realize that their character converted into an NPC because they've been programmed by the game to believe they're still playing, but they're stuck on autopilot.

Some of the NPCs we need to harvest coins, but most of the distractions are there to get you to take on smaller missions and compare your character stuff to other gear so that you'll spend coins upgrading your skins and all the other stuff.

## The Secret Grading System

Once you reach the end of the game, you get access to this character grading system that the game uses. The game judges you only while you're exiting, only when you're very close to exiting and leaving the game. You get judged on these five things:

### Secret Grading System #1: Decision Perspective (Love vs. Fear)

This is the ultimate test in the game. Without ever telling you, the developers designed life on Earth to evaluate how often you can make choices rooted in love, empathy, connection, and compassion versus all those that are driven by fear, like self-protection, judgment, and isolation. The highest rated characters navigate the game from love a lot more often, even when fear seems like a really easy path.

### Secret Grading System #2: Connection to Others

Points are scored for collaboration, deep understanding, forgiveness, the ability to dissolve the illusion of separation. The strongest players understand that they're not competing but are in this together. That's the ultimate trick because when people think of the word "game," they think they're in a game. They think that there's a winner and a loser. But that's the ultimate trick: just getting this in your head and living that in your life.

### Secret Grading System #3: Adaptability

The game is going to throw endless plot twists at you. There's going to be loss and failure, unexpected change. Those people who can learn and grow from adversity score a lot higher. Players who cling too tightly to control and resist change are going to lose points like crazy and not even know it.

### Secret Grading System #4: Authenticity

There is a hidden bonus for playing your character in alignment with who you truly are. It's not about playing by society's rules or trying to achieve external success. The developers reward characters who are true to their inner voice, their passions, their purpose—which most people don't figure out until they're about to exit the game completely.

### Secret Grading System #5: Contribution

Points are tallied up for how much you give back to the game world. It could be through helping other characters, sharing knowledge, or leaving behind something that benefits future players. Basically, if you fix things for characters or improve the game map somehow, you get points for that.
